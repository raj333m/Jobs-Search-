Privacy notice

Who runs this app. Your Next Move Awaits is a personal pilot project run by Aishwarya Raj Singh. It never submits a job application on your behalf.

What is stored. Your resume text, the profile details you confirm, your search preferences, the jobs you save or mark as applied, and your consent. Nothing is stored until you tick the consent box.

How it is protected. Your data is encrypted (AES-256) before it is saved. The app is hosted on Vercel with an Upstash database; these servers may be outside India. Your data is not sold or shared, and no external AI service receives it. Job searches send only job titles and city preferences to employer career sites, never your resume.

How long it is kept. Until you delete it, and automatically removed 12 months after your last update. A cookie links this browser to your data, so if you clear cookies or switch device you will start fresh.

How to delete your data. Type DELETE and click Delete all my data. Once you click it, your data is permanently removed from this application straight away, including your resume, profile, saved jobs and history. This cannot be undone.
